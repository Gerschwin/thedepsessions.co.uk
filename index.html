Privacy Policy

The Dep Sessions respects your privacy. This notice explains how we use any information you share with us.

What we collect: We only collect what you provide — typically your name and email address, and optionally your instrument or location if you choose to share them.

How we use it: We use your details only to keep you updated about our launch and progress, and to reply to any messages you send us.

We will never sell, share, or pass your details to anyone else.

Storage & security: Your data is stored securely and only accessed by people running The Dep Sessions.

Your rights: You can ask us to remove your details at any time — just email us at thedepsessionsscotland@gmail.com.
