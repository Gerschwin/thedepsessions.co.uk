Privacy Policy

The Dep Sessions respects your privacy. This notice explains how we use any information you share with us.

What we collect: We only collect what you provide — typically your name and email address, and optionally your instrument or location if you choose to share them.

How we use it: We use your details only to keep you updated about our launch and progress, to manage registrations and to reply to any messages you send us.

We will never sell, share, or pass your details to anyone else. We only share personal details with third parties where required by law, or if you explicitly ask us to (for example, to facilitate an event), and only with your informed consent.

Storage & security: Your data is stored securely and only accessed by people running The Dep Sessions. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

Your rights: You can ask us to remove your details at any time — just email us at thedepsessionsscotland@gmail.com. You may also request a copy of the information we hold about you.
